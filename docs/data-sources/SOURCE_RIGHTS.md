# Software and source-data rights

## Repository software

No software licence has yet been selected by the copyright holder. Until an
explicit licence is adopted, the repository software is all rights reserved.
Public access to the repository does not grant permission to use, copy, modify,
or distribute its software.

Selecting and publishing a software licence is an explicit maintainer decision
and release gate. Automated tooling must not infer, insert, or advertise a
licence before that decision is recorded.

## Medicine source data

A future software licence will apply only to material for which the repository
copyright holder can grant rights. It will not grant rights in third-party
source data, terminology, documents, identifiers, or other governed content.

In particular, NZULM, NZMT, SNOMED CT, AMT, RxNorm source vocabularies, regulator
and funder datasets, product information, and other acquired resources retain
their own licences, terms of use, access controls, attribution requirements,
and redistribution restrictions.

Each source must be assessed independently before acquisition, retention,
transformation, publication, or redistribution. The source catalog and
acquisition evidence must record:

- the official authority and source URL;
- the applicable licence or rights status;
- authentication, subscription, or approval requirements;
- whether source bytes and derived outputs may be retained or redistributed;
- required attribution and notices;
- the terms and evidence verification date.

Unknown or unresolved rights must fail closed. Restricted source bytes must not
be committed, included in releases, or published in datasets merely because
their metadata or transformations are present in this repository.
