# Australian benefits release field contract

The [versioned field contract](../../quality/qualifications/australian-benefits-release-field-contract-20261028.json)
freezes delivery requirements under the approved field-level completion policy.
It adds no sources, removes no required outputs, and preserves every frozen
source family in the qualification denominator. It is a requirements baseline,
not a qualification receipt or an amendment to Stable v1 acceptance.

Every observed field must remain source-faithful and accounted for as
`interpreted`, `native_unresolved`, or `unavailable`, with its native identity,
lineage and evidence or unresolved reason. Unavailable requested fields are
reported separately from observed-field counts. Header meanings do not promote
cell values; a typed parse or mapping destination does not establish meaning.
Overlapping semantic overlays are not additive to the field partition.

API, CLI, Atlas and deterministic export must expose bounded native observations,
field states, provenance, coverage and limits against the same admitted output.
Unsupported pooling, current status, historical equivalence, patient rates,
payable benefit, patient cost and clinical eligibility must be rejected or
explicitly unavailable. Native unresolved values can be displayed and counted
as source observations. This is useful delivery without an unsupported domain
claim; it does not declare every Silver interpretation complete.

## Evidence baseline

The contract binds existing reports by file digest. All 91 historical MBS
workbooks remain required. Their 728 header bindings have eight supported
source-column meanings; all 310,176,929 non-header values remain unresolved.
The exact PBS r19 field partition has 18,208,758 fields: 3,096,735 interpreted,
15,112,023 native unresolved, and zero unavailable. Generic qualified syntax
roles in that partition do not establish full PBS domain semantics. Historical
candidate rights flags are not rewritten; the separate exact-output rights
attestations remain independently bound.

## Conditional semantics

Interpretation work follows the claim made by a consumer:

- Converted values and era comparisons require exact source-era rules. P7 dates
  retain their ambiguity unless an applicable rule is supported.
- PBS domain claims require source-bound domain meaning, beyond a labelled path
  or typed value. Missing current-status evidence remains unavailable.
- Broader relationships require reviewed real examples, negative controls and
  calibration bound to the actual matcher. Literal matches remain diagnostics.
  Abstention does not prevent native-field delivery. The already accepted exact
  same-record MBS relationship set retains its narrow existing scope.
- Advertised historical comparisons require qualified snapshots, complete
  declared cohort denominators, temporal roles and missing-source limits.

Exact output identity, qualification, rights, publication, admission and consumer
canaries remain separate. This contract neither executes nor bypasses them.
Independent recovery, compatibility, M-105/M-106/M-107 dispositions, M-108 and
Stable v1 release conditions retain their existing obligations. Existing exact
approvals are reused within their recorded scope; no new approval or outbound
communication is requested.
