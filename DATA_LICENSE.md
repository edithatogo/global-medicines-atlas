# Licence for eligible derived data

Maintainer-owned derived datasets explicitly identified by an approved public
artifact manifest are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(`CC-BY-4.0`). Attribution must identify Global Medicines Atlas, Edith Atogo,
the exact dataset version, and its persistent identifier when available.

This licence does not apply automatically to repository files, source payloads,
terminologies, regulator or funder records, product information, third-party
identifiers, or transformations in which third-party rights subsist. Those
materials retain their source-specific terms. An output is eligible only when
the public artifact manifest identifies it as maintainer-owned and
redistributable after source-by-source rights review.

Unknown, mixed, or restricted rights fail closed. The Apache-2.0 software
licence in `LICENSE` does not grant rights in medicine data.
