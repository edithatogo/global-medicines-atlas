# Canada Vigilance public bulk archive

Unmodified official compressed ASCII extract and acquisition receipt.
Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.
Source: Health Canada. https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada

Suspected adverse reaction reports cannot by themselves establish causation,
incidence or a product safety profile. No clinical interpretation is provided.
Temporary signed-query credentials are redacted from the retrieval receipt;
the public source URI and immutable payload digest remain available.
