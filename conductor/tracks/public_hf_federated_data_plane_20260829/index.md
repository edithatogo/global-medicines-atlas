GitHub parent: [#340](https://github.com/edithatogo/global-medicines-atlas/issues/340).

This track makes public Hugging Face datasets the durable data plane for every
publication-approved Australian raw and derived object while keeping
publication, rights, sensitivity, and restore evidence independent.

Current M-108 and M-112 product status is in the
[Australian federation acceptance register](../../../docs/qualification/australian-health-federation-acceptance.md)
and the [August projection metadata and privacy update](../../../docs/qualification/australian-mbs-august-projection-replay-pbs-privacy-20261115.md).
The [eight-object admission preparation](../../../docs/qualification/australian-mbs-august-projection-admission-preparation-20261116.md)
assembles draft components without admitting or reading any payload.
The [bounded semantic and consumer checks](../../../docs/qualification/australian-mbs-august-bounded-semantics-consumers-20261116.md)
compare retained aggregate profiles and exercise offline admission isolation.
The [historical coverage reconciliation](../../../docs/qualification/australian-historical-coverage-reconciliation-20261116.md)
joins retained PBS discovery and MBS index metadata to the observed archive
revisions without claiming a complete historical denominator.
The [existing-evidence closure](../../../docs/qualification/australian-existing-evidence-closure-20261116.md)
binds each August projection to the applicable historical primary-restore and
publication evidence, and joins PBS schedule paths to their privacy context.
The [frozen projection v1 and v3 profile](../../../docs/qualification/australian-frozen-mbs-projection-v1-v3-20261116.md)
adds exact-identity role verification and compact metadata-replay receipts;
output privacy, actual admission and consumer checks remain separate.
The [5 October derived-product reconciliation](../../../docs/qualification/australian-m108-derived-products.md)
and [inventory](../../../quality/qualifications/australian-m108-derived-products-20261005.json)
remain historical evidence.

The [15 November existing-archive reconciliation](../../../docs/qualification/australian-federation-existing-archive-20261115.md)
records the retained archive identities and their evidence boundaries. The
[13 November MBS and PBS readback](../../../docs/qualification/australian-public-archive-reconciliation-20261113.md)
remains a dated snapshot with later updates identified in the document.
