**Subject:** Public item-level MBS patient-count statistics and reuse terms

Hello,

We are qualifying public MBS statistical sources for Global Medicines Atlas.
The public Services Australia reporting description says MBS item reports can
show item-level statistics and patient-demographic reports, while the
catalogued data.gov.au item-demographics resource exposes fields for item,
state, age range, gender, services, and benefit. We have not acquired either
dataset. The catalogued resource's data last-updated date is August 2016, and
its published dictionary does not identify a distinct unique-patient count.

Could you clarify, for existing public reports or datasets only:

- whether a publicly available series provides a count of distinct patients
  by MBS item and period, or whether the item-level measure is services only;
- the periods, historical coverage, denominator and suppression rules of any
  such public patient measure;
- whether the catalogued item-demographics dataset remains maintained or has a
  current successor; and
- the applicable reuse, attribution, retention and redistribution terms for
  the public outputs and any machine-readable download.

We are not requesting a custom or restricted extraction, patient-level
records, credentials, or access to non-public data. We will not acquire or
publish a source until its exact identity, rights and project-specific
authorization are established.

For reference: [MBS item reports](https://medicarestatistics.humanservices.gov.au/VEA0032/SAS.Web/statistics/mbs_item.html),
[catalogued MBS item-demographics resource](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/medicare-benefits-schedule-mbs-group-by-patient-demographics-report/resource/492b39de-8c97-4bbf-880e-e97d933daa9c),
and [Services Australia statistics contact](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/health-professionals-contact-information?context=20).

Thank you,
Global Medicines Atlas
