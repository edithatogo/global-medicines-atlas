# OSF preregistration cover: Global Medicines Atlas

> Offline rehearsal only. Status: `draft_not_submitted`. This document has not
> been submitted or registered, and it does not authorize an external action.

## Summary

A prospective, descriptive study of source-supported medicine regulatory and public-funding status across jurisdictions, with explicit comparison validity, provenance, coverage, uncertainty, and rights boundaries.

## Research questions

- How does source-supported regulatory status vary for comparable medicine entities across included jurisdictions and times?
- How does source-supported public funding status vary independently for those comparable entities across included jurisdictions and times?
- How do source coverage, mapping validity, missingness, and conflicts affect those descriptive comparisons?

## Structured registration responses

### Study Type

Observational, descriptive, secondary analysis of governed public administrative medicine sources.

### Hypotheses

No confirmatory hypothesis tests or causal hypotheses are preregistered; analyses are descriptive.

### Design

Repeated cross-jurisdiction status comparisons using bitemporal assertions and M-090 validity states.

### Sampling Plan

The denominator is the versioned source census and eligibility rules in the academic protocol, not an inferred global universe.

### Variables

Regulatory and funding status are separate outcomes; entity, indication, population, time, mapping, provenance, and uncertainty qualify each comparison.

### Analysis Plan

Use the committed Phase 2 analysis plan, report denominators and unknowns, and label any unplanned analysis exploratory.

### Stopping Rule

Freeze the preregistered source census and software identities at approved registration; later additions are prospective amendments.

## Outcome boundary

Regulatory approval and public funding are separate outcomes. Absence of an
observation is insufficient evidence, not a negative status. No clinical,
causal, individual-patient, or exhaustive-global-coverage claim is permitted.
