# Data management and ethics statement

The study processes administrative medicine information and no individual
participant data. It is descriptive, not clinical decision support. The
repository retains versioned contracts, schemas, lawful fixtures, provenance,
checksums, amendments, and deviations. Restricted or rights-unknown payloads
are not redistributed; only rights-cleared source metadata and derived outputs
may enter public packages. Final ethics applicability, licence selection, and
public wording remain maintainer gates before submission.
